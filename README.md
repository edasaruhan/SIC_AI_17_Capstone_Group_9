# AI AD-LAUNCH EMOTION ANALYZER

## Project Overview

AI AD-LAUNCH EMOTION ANALYZER is a proposed AI-based marketing analytics project designed to analyze participants' emotional responses and attention patterns during digital product launches and advertising presentations.

The project aims to transform participants' reactions into measurable insights. During a digital product presentation, participants' facial expressions and attention patterns are planned to be analyzed to understand which parts of the presentation attract attention and where interest may decrease.

## Marketing Problem

Traditional surveys and post-presentation feedback may not always reflect participants' immediate reactions.

Physical product launches and focus groups can also involve high logistical costs and geographical limitations.

This project explores whether facial emotion analysis and attention-related analysis can provide additional insights for digital product presentations and launch optimization.
